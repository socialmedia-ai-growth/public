# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: {{DATE}}**

This Privacy Policy explains how **{{APP_NAME}}** ("the App"), operated by {{YOUR_NAME_OR_ENTITY}} ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you connect your TikTok account.

## 1. Summary

The App is a personal analytics tool. It reads information about your own TikTok account and the videos you have already published, and displays it to you. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it to train machine learning models.

## 2. Information we access

When you authorize the App through TikTok, we may access the following, depending on the permissions you grant:

**Profile information** (`user.info.basic`, `user.info.profile`, `user.info.stats`)
- Your TikTok open ID and union ID
- Display name, username, and avatar image URL
- Follower count, following count, total likes, and total video count

**Video information** (`video.list`)
- Video IDs, titles, and descriptions
- Creation timestamps, durations, cover image URLs, and share URLs
- Engagement metrics: view counts, like counts, comment counts, and share counts

**Authentication data**
- OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens issued by TikTok

We access only content you have already published to your own account. We do not access your drafts, private messages, contacts, email address, phone number, payment information, or the accounts of other users.

## 3. What we do not collect

The App does not collect your TikTok password. Authentication happens entirely on TikTok's own servers through OAuth, and your credentials are never visible to us.

## 4. How the information is used

Information is used solely to:

- Authenticate your session with TikTok
- Retrieve and display your published content and its performance metrics
- Refresh your session so you do not have to re-authorize on every use

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, resale, or any purpose beyond providing the functionality described above.

## 6. Retention

Access tokens issued by TikTok expire approximately 24 hours after issue. Refresh tokens expire after approximately 365 days, or sooner if you revoke access.

Cached video and profile data is retained only as long as needed to display it to you and is overwritten on each refresh. You can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the App's local data files (Option A) or by contacting us to request deletion (Option B).

## 7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it with third parties, except:

- **TikTok**, as an inherent part of making API requests on your behalf.
- **Where legally required**, in response to a valid legal process.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels within the App.

## 8. Your rights and choices

You may at any time:

- **Revoke access.** Go to TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions, and remove the App. This immediately ends our ability to retrieve new data.
- **Request deletion.** Contact us at the address below and we will delete any data associated with your account that we hold.
- **Request a copy.** Ask us what data we hold about you.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including rights to access, correct, port, or erase your personal data, and to object to certain processing. We honor these requests regardless of your location.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a minor has connected an account, contact us and we will delete the associated data.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored tokens and data, including restricting access and avoiding transmission of credentials. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your tokens have been exposed, revoke the App's access in your TikTok settings immediately.

## 11. International transfers

If data is transferred outside your country of residence, we take steps to ensure it receives a comparable level of protection as required by applicable law.

The App is an independent project and is not created by, endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance Ltd. TikTok's own handling of your data is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy, which we do not control.
